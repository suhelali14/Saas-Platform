# Clinic plan — narration

One line per scene. Short enough to say over the scene it belongs to; long
enough to carry the thing the scene is showing. Blanks are pauses that
match the title card or the section card.

---

**[INTRO]**  
Nayaversse Clinic, in the plan a single-doctor practice runs on.
The queue, the consultation, the record, the bill, the website.
This is what runs in Sangli today.

**[SECTION 1 — The day at a glance]**

**Overview card.**  
This is the first screen the doctor sees. Follow-ups due, booking
requests, today's queue — on one page, not three.

**Queue today.**  
Everyone in today, sorted the way they'll be seen. The declared priority
outranks arrival order, so the desk stops arbitrating turns.

**Queue — emergency.**  
A chest-pain patient walks in. They jump to the top automatically. Nobody
has to argue.

**[SECTION 2 — The consultation]**

**Opening the encounter.**  
The doctor opens the visit. Vitals, case sheet, prescription, certificate
— all on one screen, against one appointment.

**Case sheet.**  
The complaint, the examination, the diagnosis, the follow-up. Templates
for common presentations, but a doctor can always type.

**Prescription.**  
The medicine autocomplete runs against a two-lakh-forty-six-thousand-row
Indian medicine database. Dose, frequency, duration — in words a
pharmacist reads.

**Certificate.**  
Sick leave, fitness or referral. Autofilled from the visit, printed to
the clinic's letterhead.

**[SECTION 3 — The patient record]**

**Opening a patient.**  
The one place every visit is on one screen.

**Day by day.**  
Each day is one card. What they came for, what was found, what was
prescribed, what was tested, what was paid. Not forty rows interleaved
by the minute.

**Lab orders.**  
A test ordered during the visit shows on the same day card when the
result comes back.

**[SECTION 4 — Billing and the day]**

**Billing desk.**  
Today's invoices, each ready to settle. Reception works from here.

**One invoice.**  
Lines, a discount, part payments across cash, UPI and card. Earnings
count money received, never invoices raised.

**Daily close.**  
What came in today, by payment mode. The number the doctor checks
before locking up.

**Access log.**  
Who read or changed which record, and when. Append-only. Built for what
DPDP and NABH ask for.

**[SECTION 5 — The clinic online]**

**Website builder.**  
The sections the patient site shows. Reorder them, add a hero image,
set a map link. No code.

**The patient site.**  
Server-rendered, so search engines read it. In up to twelve Indian
languages. And the live queue is on it, so people can watch their turn
coming without ringing the desk.

**[OUTRO]**  
That is the Clinic plan. Every part demonstrated here runs in Sangli
today. For a polyclinic, the plan above this one carries this, plus
several doctors, a pharmacy and the clinic's own expenses.
