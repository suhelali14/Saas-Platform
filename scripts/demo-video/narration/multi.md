# Multi-specialty plan — narration

Reads on top of the first three videos. Everything those plans do still
works; this walks the viewer through what a multi-specialty hospital adds.

---

**[INTRO]**  
The Multi-specialty plan. Everything Clinic, Polyclinic and Hospital do
still works. This is what a multi-specialty hospital adds: the
government-scheme desk, radiology, the nursing stations and the
specialty records engine every specialty shares.

**[SECTION 1 — Government schemes]**

**A scheme claim.**  
An MJPJAY case. Beneficiary, the package code and amount from the
scheme's current circular, the pre-authorisation status, the documents
the scheme asks for, settlement.

**Scheme packages.**  
The hospital's own copy of each scheme's rates. Updated from the
circular the scheme publishes — rates change and a claim billed from an
old list is a wrong claim.

**[SECTION 2 — Radiology]**

**Imaging worklist.**  
Every scan ordered by a doctor, on three tabs: to do, to report,
reported. The radiographer works from this screen.

**The report.**  
Findings, impression, who reported it, when. The order flips to
reported only once the report is in — not before.

**[SECTION 3 — Nursing stations]**

**Ward list.**  
Every admitted patient, ward by ward, with how long since vitals were
last charted. Overdue rows are marked. The round at a glance.

**[SECTION 4 — Specialty records]**

**A pregnancy.**  
One record per pregnancy. LMP, EDD worked out automatically, the
obstetric score, risk flags. Each antenatal visit adds a row to the
card — weight, BP, fundal height, haemoglobin — and the card prints
on demand.

**Each visit is a row.**  
Thirty-eight weeks and seven antenatal rows. Trends are drawn from
the card's own data — no second screen to copy the numbers to.

**A fertility couple.**  
The couple's record lives on one file and links the partner. Either
partner's own tests sit on the couple's card, from whichever file
they were recorded on.

**A growing child.**  
Birth history, every dose on the IAP schedule, and a growth visit
with weight and height trended against age. A paediatrician works
from this.

**The form library.**  
Every specialty's forms. An admin can switch one off, add the
clinic's own questions to a form, or build a new form from scratch —
an ENT clinic's audiometry, an endocrinology thyroid review. A field
taken away is kept hidden on the server, so old records still read.

**[OUTRO]**  
That is the Multi-specialty plan. Built and tested on a demo hospital;
no hospital runs it in production yet. The clinic plan does, and every
part of it is in Sangli today.
