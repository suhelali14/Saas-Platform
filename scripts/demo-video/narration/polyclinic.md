# Polyclinic plan — narration

Reads on top of the Clinic plan video. Everything the Clinic plan does
still works; this walks the viewer through what a polyclinic adds.

---

**[INTRO]**  
The Polyclinic plan. Everything the Clinic plan does — the queue, the
consultation, the record, the bill — still works. This walkthrough is
what a polyclinic adds on top: a second doctor, a pharmacy, the day's
money, your website in Indian languages.

**[SECTION 1 — Several doctors]**

**Two doctors on the roster.**  
A booking is against a doctor. The queue filters to whoever is signed
in, so each doctor sees only their own patients.

**The other doctor's queue.**  
Sign in as the paediatrician — different queue, same screen. The
appointment carries the doctor.

**Staff list.**  
A login per person. Each person has one job, one or many desks, and
their own access. The audit log can name who did what because of this.

**[SECTION 2 — The pharmacy]**

**Pharmacy desk.**  
Every prescription waiting to be dispensed. One click, and stock comes
off the right batch.

**First-expired, first-out.**  
Stock is dispensed from the batch with the soonest expiry, never from
an expired one. A short stock warns at the counter; it never blocks
dispensing. Low stock and near-expiry are visible before they bite.

**[SECTION 3 — Expenses and the P&L]**

**Expense ledger.**  
What the clinic spends. Fixed categories, so the same cost cannot be
typed three ways and the totals still add up.

**Profit and loss.**  
Earnings count money received, never invoices raised. What is
outstanding sits on its own list, not folded into revenue.

**[SECTION 4 — Booking requests]**

**Booking requests.**  
People who enquired on the website. Reception works down the list, books
each one against a doctor, and the enquiry is closed.

**Reports.**  
Visits by week, top diagnoses, each doctor's share of the day. Enough
for a doctor to know how the practice is running without a dashboard
team.

**[SECTION 5 — Your website in twelve Indian languages]**

**Website editor.**  
The sections your patients see — hero, services, hours, doctors,
feedback, blog. Reorder them. Pick a hero image.

**In Hindi.**  
The user interface is hand-translated into twelve languages; the
clinic's own prose is for the clinic to enter, in the languages patients
read. The example here is the Sangli clinic's content in Hindi.

**In Marathi.**  
Same page, same data, in Marathi. Server-rendered, so a shared link
unfurls and a search engine reads the Marathi content as Marathi.

**[OUTRO]**  
That is the Polyclinic plan. For a hospital, the next plan carries this,
plus the hospital desks: departments, the cash counter, inpatients, the
theatre and the insurance desk.
