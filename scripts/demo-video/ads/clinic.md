# Clinic plan — ad script

A product ad, not a walkthrough. 70-80 seconds, ten lines, one promise.
Spoken by a single voice (default: Prabhat, male, Indian English) over
captured footage from the demo clinic.

The lines below are what the voice-over says. The timing column says when
in the final cut each one starts. The B-roll column names the scene the
script exposed in tiers/clinic.mjs -- the renderer plays that scene under
that line.

Structure: open (problem), promise, three proofs, close (CTA).
